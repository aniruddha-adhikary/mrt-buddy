MRT Buddy: Your Companion for Seamless Commuting in Dhaka

Say goodbye to the hassle of unexpected low balances and keep track of your travel history effortlessly. MRT Buddy allows you to check the current balance and view up to 19 recent transactions of your Dhaka MRT and Rapid Pass transit cards—all in one convenient app.

Key Features:

- Instant Balance Updates: Get real-time information on your card balances for both Dhaka MRT and Rapid Pass systems.
- Recent Transaction History: Access up to 19 recent transactions to monitor your spending and travel patterns.
- User-Friendly Interface: Navigate with ease through a clean and intuitive design.
- Secure Access: Your data privacy is our top priority; rest assured that your information is protected.
